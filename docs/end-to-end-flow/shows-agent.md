# Live shows: end to end

What the agent does today for a single show or a short run, and what the docs promise that is not built.

## A build

1. The router reads the message. A concert, a gig or a live show matches Music and Live shows, so it asks which. Stand-up, club nights and stage plays go straight to Live shows.
2. The reader pulls the act, show type, show date, cities, capacity, price band, on-sale weeks, budget, rivals and the comparable acts. A missing field is "not available".
3. One question if there is no city, then the budget band card, then the platforms card, as in every category.
4. Qloo is asked about the act's audience, the cities, similar acts, sponsor brands, podcasts and venues. Rivals named with a date are scored in one call.
5. Code writes the on-sale and pacing section, the final push, the budget by phase, the show calendar, the content calendar, channels and what to measure. The model writes the rest, and every line is checked.
6. The plan lists a call for each rival date, partners with their tiers and flags, and the territories.

## Follow ups

What-if, rival date check, partner brief with its pitch kit, and the creative kit (for one territory, or every territory) all work as in Music. Results can be added as ticket numbers by city.

## Audit and fix

An audit tests the beliefs and sets the plan's dates and shares beside E1, E3, E4 and E6. A fix keeps the brief's rivals, band and platforms.

## Not built

- Ticket price, capacity and calendar data: Qloo holds none, so the team supplies them.
- A date check for comedians and plays as rivals: Qloo scores artists only, so such a rival is called from its date.
- Theatre and screening on-sale windows: not researched.
- A recorded live Qloo run for the goldens.
